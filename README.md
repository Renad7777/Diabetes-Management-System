🩺 Diabetes Treatment Recommendation System
📌 Overview 

The Diabetes Treatment Recommendation System is an AI-powered healthcare project designed to help diabetes patients manage their health through personalized recommendations for diet and exercise.

The system analyzes patient health data such as blood sugar level, blood pressure, cholesterol, BMI, and age to provide customized meal plans and workout recommendations that improve lifestyle and reduce diabetes complications.

❗ Problem Statement

Many diabetes patients lack personalized guidance for:

🥗 Healthy diet plans
🏃 Suitable exercise routines
📊 Daily health monitoring

Most available resources provide general advice that does not fit individual patient conditions, which may lead to poor health decisions and increased complications.
